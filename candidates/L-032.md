## Step 1 — human read of the page
Read on the scan 2026-11-09; transcription matches. Verbatim (upper two-thirds; lower third unrelated):
`p = sin σz λ` · `u = sin σz ∂λ/∂x + sin τz aτ ∂λ/∂x` · `v = … ∂λ/∂y` · `w = cos σz σλ − cos τz aϱλ` ·
`(−σ² − ϱθ − σ²θ) sin σc + aτϱ sin τc = 0` · `cos σc 2σ + cos τc a(τ² − ϱ) = 0` ·
`τ' + σ' = λ, τ' − σ' = 1/λ` · `1/(1+2θ) − σ'² = 2(1+θ)/(1+2θ) − τ'² = ϱ'` ·
`sin τc cos σc / (sin σc cos τc) = (τ'² − ϱ')[σ'²(1+θ) − ϱ'θ] / (2σ'τ'ϱ')`, then rational forms in λ² + λ⁻² and 1/r².
Gloss: plate |z| ≤ c; displacement = dilatational part + shear part with mixing amount a; the two "= 0" lines are
zero normal stress and zero shear stress on the faces; the ratio is the frequency–wavelength equation.

## Step 2 — maths checked by code
`verify/r26_228r_plate_flexural.py` (sympy): with Δ₂λ = −ϱλ and θ = λ_Lamé/(2μ), σ_zz/(2μλ) at z = c equals the
page's first equation exactly and σ_xz/(μλ_x) the second; eliminating a gives
tan τc / tan σc = −(τ² − ϱ)[σ²(1+θ) + ϱθ]/(2στϱ), which with k_T²/k_L² = 2(1+θ) is identically the antisymmetric
Rayleigh–Lamb equation tan(qh)/tan(ph) = −(k² − q²)²/(4k²pq). The page's written ratio differs by an overall sign and
the sign of the ϱ'θ term (two slips in the working; the rational forms below it carry the slips faithfully).

## Step 3 — literature search on the meaning
Rayleigh, Proc. LMS 20 (1889) 225–234: first exact free-plate frequency equations, "not limited to the case of small
thickness"; cites no earlier exact plate solution. Lamb, Proc. LMS 21 (1889), Appendix: concedes the infinite plate
to Rayleigh. Love, Treatise (1920) Arts. 333–334: credits Rayleigh only. Lamb 1917 named the waves.
Before 1866: Poisson 1829 / Cauchy 1828 — series in the thickness; Lamé 1852, 13e Leçon — exact modes with shear-free
faces only, no frequency equation (Todhunter–Pearson I, Arts. 1078–1085); Kirchhoff 1850/1876, Clebsch 1862,
Saint-Venant 1883 — thin-plate theory. Riemann: Werke — no elasticity paper; Hattendorff's lectures (1869) skip
plates ("über die Schwingungen solcher Platten hat zuerst Sophie Germain geschrieben … Kirchhoff"); Riemann–Weber
1900/1910 — none; Nachträge 1902 report on Akt 26 — no elasticity; Nachträge p. 114: elasticity courses SS 1856 and
WS 1859/60 ("nach Lamé", letter of 7 July 1857). Attribution to Riemann: **not found** (also Papadopoulos 2017,
Kalliope, web). Not accessed: Lamb 1917 introduction; Mindlin 1960; Graff.
