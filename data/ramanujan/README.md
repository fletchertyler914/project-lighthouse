# Ramanujan — sources and access (survey 2026-11-09)

- **Trinity College Cambridge, Wren Library, Add.MS a/94 "Papers of Srinivasa Ramanujan"** — fully digitised, 631 IIIF
  images, licence CC BY-NC 4.0, attribution "Trinity College, Cambridge". Viewer
  `https://mss-cat.trin.cam.ac.uk/manuscripts/uv/view.php?n=Add.Ms.a.94.N`, manifests
  `https://mss-cat.trin.cam.ac.uk/manuscripts/Add.Ms.a.94.N.json` (N = 1…18). Items: a/94/15 the Lost Notebook (148 images),
  a/94/18 "Miscellaneous manuscripts" given by Miss G. K. Stanley to Hardy, 1930 (134 images; Hardy's covering letter
  to Watson is image 1–2: "a large bundle of Ramanujan MSS, mostly fragmentary … refer to things mainly in the Papers
  or in his notebooks: but not all"; contents per Hardy: three connected blocks, the largest the suppressed part of
  "Highly Composite Numbers", plus miscellany), a/94/13 "Properties of p(n) and τ(n)" (44), letters.
  Images are linked, not hosted (non-commercial licence); the transcriptions here are of public-domain text.
- **Cambridge University Library MS Add.7011** — the five 1913–14 letters to Hardy, 63 images, CC BY-NC 4.0, IIIF.
- **Notebooks 1–3** (University of Madras originals; TIFR facsimile 1957/2011; archive.org
  `ramanujan-unpublished-notebooks-and-lost-notebook`, uploader-claimed PD; Tamil Digital Library scans of the
  originals, 2025, licence unstated).
- **Coverage baseline:** Berndt, *Ramanujan's Notebooks* I–V (1985–98, 3,254 entries); Andrews–Berndt, *Ramanujan's
  Lost Notebook* I–V (2005–18) with Part V ch. 18 "Corrections" and ch. 19 "The Continuing Mystery"; Nicolas–Robin
  1997 (the suppressed part of Highly Composite Numbers); Berndt–Ono 1999 (p(n), τ(n) manuscript); Karatsuba 2001
  (gamma-function approximation). Berndt 2018: "we think we have found proofs of all the claims in the lost notebook".
- **Odds (survey):** ~2/10 for a publishable misreading or unrecorded entry; <1/10 for a major theorem. First
  target: a/94/18, the least-examined digitised object.
